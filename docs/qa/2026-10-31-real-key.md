# QA: Real API key, user journey

**Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Tester:** Claude (by hand, with the user's key typed in by the user) · **Build:** browser preview (npm run dev), real AI: OpenAI, model `gpt-6-luna`
**Scope:** The core writing loop against a real provider: setup, first chapter, Stop, End Chapter and review, second chapter, Regenerate, wrong-model error · **Time spent:** about 30 minutes

## Summary

The app works end to end with a real provider. Blocks stream in, follow the direction, and come with three useful suggested actions. Stop, End Chapter, the review, chapter 2 openings, Regenerate and the wrong-model error all behave as the spec says. No app bug was confirmed. The issues are one setup trap (pasting OpenAI's key ID instead of the secret key gives a message that doesn't explain the real cause) and one continuity slip by the AI that the chapter summary then repeated. The whole run used about 13.5k input and 5.8k output tokens.

Test story: "QA Real Key — The Ember Road" (Fantasy, English, Short chapters, Short story). It stays in this browser's storage and can be deleted.

## Findings

### QA-01 · Minor · AI output · A detail moved from one character to another, and the summary kept it

**Steps:** Chapter 1, blocks 2 and 3 of the test story.

**Expected:** Facts stay with the character they belong to.
**Actual:** In block 2 the plague sores are on the young rider's hand (the captain threatens to burn him). In block 3 "the sores beneath his glove had reached his wrist" is said of the captain. The End Chapter summary repeats this ("the captain's sores have spread to his wrist"), so the error is now part of the story's memory.
**Note:** One slip in one run, so not a pattern yet. Worth watching in longer runs; the AI call log in `ai-log/` shows what each block was sent. The review step only proposes character changes, so the user has no way to correct a summary fact there.

### QA-02 · Low confidence · Chapter review · Approving two changes in the same instant marked only one

**Steps:** On the review, both Approve buttons were clicked by script in the same tick.
**Actual:** Only the second showed "Approved"; the first had to be approved again. After that both changes were in the character file.
**Note:** A person cannot click that fast, so this is probably not reachable by hand. If it is, the cause would be a state update built from a stale copy of the review. Not checked in code.

## Part 2: longer run, prompt, continuity and music

Same story, carried on to the end of chapter 4 (14 kept blocks, 4 End Chapter calls). Whole story so far: about 66.6k input and 25.5k output tokens. Chapter 5 is open with no blocks.

### Summary

The prompt is well built and the story holds together across chapters better than expected: equipment, wounds, items and new characters are carried forward, and the AI even reconciled the chapter 1 slip (QA-01) on its own. The two real weaknesses are the **music** and **character conditions**. The AI picks `tense` for most blocks, so music plays almost all the time instead of only at peaks (QA-03). And the End Chapter review twice proposed a condition that silently dropped Maud's plague marks, a key clue (QA-04). The player itself behaves as spec 13.2 and 13.3 say.

### The prompt (from `ai-log/`)

Layers sent for a chapter 3 block: instructions, setting, characters in the scene, story so far, scenes already used, recent chapters, end of the previous chapter, the chapter so far, direction. Good:

- Characters in the scene grow as the story does (Wren; then Wren and Maud).
- "Scenes already used" stops repeats; no scene was replayed in 4 chapters.
- The previous chapter's last ~400 words make chapter openings follow on smoothly.

Worth a look:

- After chapter 1, "The story so far" and "Recent chapters" say almost the same thing (two summaries of chapter 1). That costs tokens without adding facts. It will matter less as the story grows.
- The ready-made Fantasy world contradicts itself (QA-05).

### QA-03 · Major · Music · The AI picks `tense` for most blocks, so music almost never stops

**Fixed 2026-10-31** (decision 139): the mood rule now names what is not a peak, narrows `tense` to a fight or capture about to break out in that block, and gives a guide of about one block in four. Checked with the real AI in chapter 6: travel, two conversations and hiding from a patrol → `none` (silent); a blades-drawn standoff → `tense`; the fight → `battle`. 2 of 6 blocks with music, both at real peaks.

**Expected:** `none` for most blocks; music only at real peaks (spec 13.1).
**Actual:** 11 of 14 blocks had a peak mood, 9 of them `tense`. Ordinary suspense (hiding, a voice through a wall, reading a ledger, setting off on the road) counts as "danger close". Because `none` lets the track play on, the tense track ran under a quiet campfire scene in chapter 4. In practice the music is close to continuous, which is the opposite of the "silent most of the time" design.
**Ideas:** make the prompt stricter (for example "at most one block in four; `tense` only when a fight or capture is about to happen in this block"), give the AI the previous block's mood so it can avoid repeating it, or have `none` fade out a `tense` track after a short time.

### QA-04 · Major · Chapter review · A proposed condition replaces the old one and drops important facts

**Fixed 2026-10-31** (decision 138): the End Chapter call now says a new value replaces the old one and lasting facts must be kept. Checked with the real AI in chapter 5: Maud's proposal kept "faint pale marks on her wrists" and added the new ankle and forearm injuries.

**Steps:** End chapters 3 and 4 with Maud in the story.
**Actual:** Both times the proposal for Maud's condition was a full replacement ("Thin and cold, with split nails torn and bleeding…", then "Wet and cold, coughing…") without "faint pale marks on her wrists", the plague sign the plot turns on. Approving it would delete that fact from her character file, and so from every later prompt. I rejected it both times, but a user reading quickly would approve.
**Ideas:** tell the End Chapter prompt that a condition must keep lasting facts (marks, scars, illness) and drop only what has healed; or show what will be removed in the review.

### QA-05 · Minor · Ready-made worlds · The Fantasy "plague and ash" world contradicts its own main character

**Fixed 2026-10-31:** the `ash-and-iron` difficulties now say she is wanted "for a theft that turned out to be far worse than they knew", in English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. Stories created from this world before the fix keep their saved setting.

[fantasy.json:145](../../src/storyTypes/sets/fantasy.json) says the main character "is wanted by the church for a crime they did not commit"; [fantasy.json:162](../../src/storyTypes/sets/fantasy.json) says "Three months ago she robbed a church wagon". Both go into every prompt. Fix the data, e.g. "wanted by the church for a theft that was worse than she knew".

### QA-06 · Minor · Music · `data-music` shows silence during a crossfade

`stop()` clears `current` and reports "" before the new track's `play()` resolves, so `<html data-music>` reads empty for 1–3 s while the new mood's file loads ([music.ts](../../src/lib/music.ts) `blockFinished` → `stop` → `report`). Audio is fine apart from a possible short gap the first time a file loads. Matters for E2E tests that read the attribute right after a block.
